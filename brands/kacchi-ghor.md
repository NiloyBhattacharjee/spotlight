# Kacchi Ghor

## What it is
A family restaurant in Gulshan 1, Dhaka, serving kacchi biryani, rezala and Bangladeshi home-style lunch and dinner. You can dine in, pick up or get delivery.

Address: House 12, Road 11, Gulshan 1, Dhaka 1212.

## Who it's for
- Families and groups who want to book a table for lunch or dinner
- People in Gulshan, Banani and Baridhara who want food delivered
- People who want to order ahead and pick up
- Customers who write in Bangla or English

## What it does for them
**Food**
- Mutton Kacchi Biryani: basmati rice and mutton slow-cooked together, with potato and an egg, in half or full
- Chicken Roast: quarter chicken in a sweet and spicy roast gravy
- Beef Rezala: beef in a mild, milky white curry
- Borhani: spiced yoghurt drink
- Firni: chilled rice pudding in a clay bowl

**Table bookings**
- Book up to 30 days ahead
- Book for up to 12 people; for bigger groups, the team handles the request
- The last booking is 60 minutes before closing

**Pickup and delivery**
- Pickup: collect at the front desk and mention your name
- Delivery: Gulshan, Banani and Baridhara only; allow 45-60 minutes
- Pay by cash on delivery (or cash at pickup) or bKash

**Messaging**
- Ask about the menu, book a table or place an order by message, in Bangla or English
- Voice notes are accepted and answered with a voice note; photos (JPG/PNG) are accepted too
- You can ask to talk to a real person

**Parking**
- Parking in front for 6 cars, with more on Road 11

**Opening hours (Dhaka time)**
- Monday to Thursday and Sunday: 12:00-16:00 and 18:00-23:00
- Friday: 13:30-16:00 and 18:00-23:30
- Saturday: 12:00-16:00 and 18:00-23:30
