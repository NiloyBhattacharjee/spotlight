# Mitu

## What it is
Mitu is an AI assistant for shops that sell in DMs on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. It answers customers' questions and takes their orders in chat, day and night, and hands off to a person when it should. It comes with a dashboard where staff see orders, chats and products.

## Who it's for
- **Main audience:** small sellers on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp who have no website or checkout, so every sale happens in DMs.
- **Their daily problem:** answering the same messages over and over: "price?", "is M available?", "delivery charge outside Dhaka?", "COD?"
- **Also:** cafés that take orders, and restaurants that take table bookings as well as orders.

## What it does for them
**Answers customers**
- Replies day and night about prices, sizes, colours, stock left and delivery charges.
- Replies in the language the customer writes or speaks in.
- Understands voice notes.
- Understands photos, such as a screenshot of a post with "how much is this one?"
- Hands the chat to a person when it should.

**Takes orders**
- Takes orders for delivery or pickup, with the payment methods the business sets, such as cash on delivery or bKash.
- Takes orders for today or, for items set up that way, a later day.
- Counts stock down on tracked items, so the last one is never sold twice. A cancelled order puts the stock back.
- Sends one reminder when a customer hasn't answered an order summary.
- Books tables for businesses that turn on reservations.

**Works where customers are**
- WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram.
- SMS and phone calls too, so customers on any mobile phone can text or call with no app and no data.

**Keeps the owner in control (dashboard)**
- Live chats: staff can reply, use saved replies, and switch the AI on or off for each chat.
- Live orders in New / Preparing / Ready columns, with a sound when one arrives and a button to pause online orders.
- Every order listed, with CSV export.
- Revenue per day, orders, cancellations, top items, pickup vs delivery, share handled by AI and AI cost, compared with the previous period.
- Product management: prices, options, stock, photos and a sold-out switch.

**Optional**
- Voice-note replies to customers who send voice notes, with the same words as text (WhatsApp only for real customer chats).

## Tone of voice
Plain, friendly and practical, like one shop owner talking to another. Use short sentences and real DM moments ("price?", "COD?", "is M available?"). Show the problem, then Mitu handling it. Avoid technical jargon.

## Look

## Language
English. Short customer messages like the ones above work well as on-screen examples.

## Never say
- Any customer numbers, sales increases, time saved, response times, ratings or testimonials.
- That Nakshi Threads is a real customer. It is a demo business.
- Any price, "free", free trial or discount.
- Guarantees such as "never makes mistakes", "100% accurate", "never miss a sale" or "replaces your staff".
- That it understands every language perfectly. Bangla speech is weaker than English.
- That voice replies work on Messenger or Instagram.
- That Mitu processes or collects payments. It takes orders with the chosen payment method.
- That it builds a website or online checkout.
- That setup is instant or one-click, or needs no verification. A real WhatsApp business number needs Meta business verification.
- That Mitu is endorsed by or partnered with Meta, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or bKash.
- Competitors by name.
