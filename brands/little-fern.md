# Little Fern Café

## What it is
A neighbourhood café at 42 King Street, Newtown NSW 2042. It serves specialty coffee, all-day breakfast and lunch, and dinner on Thursday to Saturday. You can dine in, pick up or get local delivery.

## Who it's for
- Locals in Newtown, and for delivery, people within 3 km of the café (Newtown, Enmore, Erskineville, Camperdown)
- Coffee drinkers
- People after breakfast or lunch, or dinner from Thursday to Saturday
- People who want to order today for pickup or delivery
- People looking for vegetarian or vegan options

## What it does for them
**Visit or order**
- Dine in, pick up or get local delivery
- Pickup is free: collect at the counter and mention your name
- Pickup and delivery orders are for today only, and must be for a time within opening hours
- The earliest an order can be ready is 15 minutes after it's placed
- Delivery takes about 30–45 minutes
- Pay in person by card or cash, at pickup or to the driver

**Coffee and drinks**
- Flat White, Latte, Cappuccino and Long Black, in regular or large
- Oat, almond and soy milk for the Flat White, Latte and Cappuccino
- Decaf and extra shot available on those four coffees
- House-brewed spiced Chai Latte
- Iced Latte: double shot over ice and cold milk
- Fresh Orange Juice, squeezed to order

**Food**
- Smashed Avo on Sourdough: avocado, feta, cherry tomatoes, dukkah and lemon
- Big Breakfast: two eggs your way, bacon, pork sausage, mushrooms, roast tomato, hash brown and sourdough
- Granola Bowl: house toasted granola, coconut yoghurt, berries and maple
- Banana Bread: toasted, with whipped butter
- Fried Chicken Burger: buttermilk fried chicken, slaw, pickles and chipotle mayo on a brioche bun, with chips
- Roast Pumpkin Salad: with quinoa, rocket, pepitas, goat's cheese and balsamic
- Mushroom Risotto: wild mushroom and truffle with parmesan (Thursday to Saturday from 5pm)
- Almond Croissant: twice-baked, filled with frangipane

**Dietary labels on the menu**
- Vegan: Long Black (without added milk), Fresh Orange Juice, Granola Bowl
- Vegetarian: Smashed Avo, Banana Bread, Roast Pumpkin Salad, Mushroom Risotto, Almond Croissant
- Gluten-free: Fresh Orange Juice, Roast Pumpkin Salad, Mushroom Risotto
- Allergen note, to go with any dietary mention: "Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs and soy, so we can't guarantee any dish is free of them."

**Opening hours**
- Monday to Wednesday: 7am–3pm
- Thursday and Friday: 7am–9pm
- Saturday: 8am–9pm
- Sunday: 8am–3pm

**Getting there**
- Street parking on King St
- Newtown station is a 5-minute walk

## Never say
- That any dish is allergen-free, nut-free, dairy-free or safe for people with allergies or coeliac disease
- That the Smashed Avo is gluten-free, even with gluten-free bread
- Anything promoting the Lemon Tart, which is currently sold out
- That dinner or the Mushroom Risotto is available Sunday to Wednesday
- That you can pre-order for another day
- That delivery is free, goes beyond 3 km or is guaranteed to arrive within a set time
- That you can pay online
- That bookings or catering are offered
- "Best", "award-winning", "famous" or "favourite"
- Claims about bean origin, roasting, or organic, local or sustainable sourcing
- That everything is made in-house (only describe the chai as house-brewed and the granola as house toasted)
- Any invented reviews, ratings, customer numbers, discounts or guarantees
- Competitors by name
